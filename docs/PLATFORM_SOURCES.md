# AI interview platforms: sources and scope

Reviewed 15 September 2026. This section adds six platform guides and twelve original practice exercises to the existing repository and foundation library. Platform facts use official candidate-facing pages. Preparation plans and question selections are InterviewPrep editorial advice, not an official rubric, actual question bank or promise of selection. No application was submitted and no external interview was started.

## Mercor

Mercor documents an AI interviewer asking role-specific questions and producing a transcript for evaluation.

Its help page contains conflicting statements about practice availability. Check your current dashboard for practice, retakes and the instructions for your assigned interview.

- [Official AI interview guide](https://talent.docs.mercor.com/support/ai-interview)
- [Application process](https://talent.docs.mercor.com/how-to/apply)

## micro1

micro1’s guide describes role-based technical and scenario questions, with coding or human-data exercises where applicable. It also links a dedicated practice tool.

Questions adapt to your background and selected expertise. A certificate or completed interview is not a promise of a project; use your dashboard for current next steps.

- [Official interview guide](https://www.micro1.ai/ai-interview-guide)
- [Official practice tool](https://www.micro1.ai/interview-prep)
- [Published question categories](https://www.micro1.ai/interview-questions)

## Alignerr

Alignerr’s process page says job-specific steps may include a Zara AI interview or a domain assessment.

An additional interview or assessment is conditional on the project. Follow the instructions sent for your opportunity rather than assuming one universal sequence.

- [Official application process](https://www.alignerr.com/process)

## Outlier

Outlier describes skill screenings followed by project-specific onboarding and assessment tasks. Work can include prompts, response ranking and answer improvement.

Treat the assigned project guidelines as the assessment contract. This is not a promise that every Outlier opportunity uses a conversational AI interview or has tasks available.

- [Official contributor FAQ](https://outlier.ai/faq)

## Turing

Turing’s published developer guide describes technical vetting, including coding evaluation and discussion of developer skills.

Stages depend on the role and invitation. Technical hiring assessments are not necessarily a conversational AI interview. Consult your current application rather than relying on older timing or retake claims.

- [Official developer interview guide](https://www.turing.com/blog/turing-com-review-good-for-remote-developer-jobs)

## HireVue

HireVue’s candidate FAQ describes on-demand video interviews and assessment question types such as scenarios and past behavior.

The employer’s invitation determines the format and rules. HireVue is interview software used by employers, not an open AI-work marketplace or a guarantee that every interview uses the same assessment.

- [Official candidate FAQ](https://www.hirevue.com/candidates/faq)
- [Official on-demand interview guide](https://www.hirevue.com/blog/hiring/video-interviewing-guide)

## Updating this section

Edit the platforms array in data/content.json. Each guide has a stable ID, kind, summary, sourced format, current-invitation check, preparation steps, focus areas, source links, review date and a questionIds reading list. These lists can include existing project and foundation questions. Validate every reference with node scripts/validate.mjs. Do not turn interview categories into claims that an exact question will appear.

Mercor’s current help page is internally inconsistent about practice availability; the guide exposes that uncertainty and directs readers to their dashboard. Turing’s linked developer guide is older process documentation, so the site avoids fixed duration, retake and stage-count claims. Rates, country eligibility and current openings are not tracked by this site.
